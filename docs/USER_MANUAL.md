# End-Panel Sign Studio — User Manual

## 1. Getting started
- Open the studio. The left panel is the editor; the right side is a live, true-to-size preview.
- A short tour appears on your first visit. Everything you design stays in your browser — no account needed.
- Use **Undo / Redo** (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) freely. Switch the editor language (English, Español, Français) from the toolbar.

## 2. The Content tab
- **Collection preset**: pick Adult Fiction, Nonfiction, Young Adult, Mid Grade, Kids, Genealogy, Holds or a Library of Congress preset to fill in sensible titles, ranges, icons and colors.
- **Library header**: your library's name across the top. Toggle it off for a cleaner sign.
- **Title** and **primary call-number range**: the two most important lines. Keep the range short, e.g. `FIC A – G` or `500 – 599`.
- **Sub-ranges**: one line per Dewey range, LC class or genre.
- **Aisle number**, **floor / zone tag**, **arrow** and **footer note** are optional wayfinding helpers.
- **Icon**: a small symbol to help visitors recognize the section at a glance.
- **QR code** and **Braille reference**: placed automatically so they never cover text, the footer, or each other.
- **Text direction**: choose right-to-left for Arabic, Hebrew and similar scripts.

## 3. The Template & Style tab
- Choose one of five looks: **Modern Civic Bold**, **Editorial Classic**, **Wayfinding Split**, **Playful Geometric**, or **Full-Bleed Image**.
- Set primary, accent, text and background colors. **Reset to collection default** restores the preset palette.
- A contrast warning appears if text is hard to read against its background — fix it before printing.
- Pick heading and body fonts, including Noto fonts for non-Latin scripts. Size sliders control title, call number and body text.
- **Background image**: drag in a PNG, JPG or WebP. Adjust fit, focal point, tint color, tint strength and blur so text stays legible. Use images at least 150 pixels per inch of sign width.

## 4. The Size & Layout tab
- Presets: Letter, Tabloid, standard 12" × 18" end panel, narrow 6" × 18", 18" × 24" poster, and A4–A1.
- **Custom size**: any width and height from 3" to 48". Switch between inches and centimeters.
- **Orientation** swaps width and height. **Padding** and **Border** control the frame.
- A viewing-distance hint tells you whether your text is large enough to read from across the room.

## 5. Saved signs and batches
- Type a name and press **Save**. Saved signs can be loaded, duplicated or deleted.
- **Export JSON / Import JSON** moves your saved signs to another computer.
- **CSV import** builds a whole aisle run at once. Columns: `title, range, subranges, aisle, zone, footer`. Rows with no range are skipped.
- **All as one PDF** puts every saved sign on its own page.
- **Library defaults** remember your header and footer for every new sign.

## 6. Sharing with other branches (optional)
- Sign in from the Saved tab to create a library workspace or join one with an invite code.
- Shared signs are visible only to members of that library. Local signs remain on your computer either way.

## 7. AI wording helper (optional, signed-in)
- Enter the collection name, call-number range and intended readers (e.g. "teens", "genealogy researchers").
- The helper suggests a title, range wording, topic lines, footer and a size hierarchy, with a short reason. Review and press **Apply** to use it.

## 8. Preflight and printing
- Before every export, **Preflight** checks for clipped text, text too close to the trim edge, characters the chosen font can't show, low-resolution backgrounds, overlapping QR/Braille, and size mismatches.
- **Export PNG** saves a 300 DPI image. **Export PDF** saves an exact-size PDF. **Print** sends just the sign to your printer — set scale to 100% / "Actual size".
- **Print-shop PDF** adds 0.125" bleed and crop marks. **Two-sided PDF** creates a back side with arrows mirrored.
- Colors are designed on screen (RGB). Ask your print shop for a proof if exact brand colors matter.

## 9. Tips for good signs
- One idea per sign: section name plus call-number range.
- Hang end panels so the range is near eye level, and use high-contrast colors.
- Keep the same template and colors across a floor so visitors learn the pattern.
- Braille shown here is a visual reference; order tactile signs from an ADA sign vendor.

## 10. Privacy
- No patron data is collected. Signs stay in your browser unless you choose to share them with your library.

## Moving, resizing and curving elements
Click any text, icon or arrow on the sign and drag it. Positions snap to the grid chosen in the **Snap grid** box (1/8" to 1", or Off). With an element selected you can change its size, nudge it with the arrow buttons, or use **Curve** to bend text along a round arc (positive arches up, negative curves down). **Reset this element** or **Reset all positions** puts things back.

## Matching colors to a photo
When you upload a background photo on the Style tab, the sign colors are picked from the photo automatically (untick "Match sign colors to uploaded photo" to keep your colors). **Match colors to this photo** re-applies it any time. Text colors are checked for readable contrast.
